# ClipsX product strategy

## Positioning

ClipsX is a powerful, local-first clipboard manager. The full local experience is free; Pro pays for deliberate preservation, hosted AI, encrypted cloud services, and intentional sharing.

## Free vs Pro

| Capability | Free | Pro |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Local clipboard history; text, image, and file clips; search; filters; OCR; semantic and image search; previews; converters; favorites; pins; snippets; and optional local models | Included | Included |
| Rich PowerPoint, Excel, and Word clip preservation and editable restoration | Limited trial | Full access |
| Hosted Claude actions: translation, rewriting, summarization, and custom transformations | — | Monthly allowance |
| Private snippets | Manually saved and encrypted locally | End-to-end encrypted sync |
| Encrypted backup and recovery for deliberately saved items | — | Included |
| Secure clip sharing and encrypted shared collections | Receive shared collections | Create, invite to, sync, and share collections |

## Privacy promise

ClipsX never uploads raw clipboard history or native Office clipboard data. Only items a user deliberately saves or shares may be uploaded. Private snippets are manually saved, redacted by default, and excluded from cloud AI and sharing unless the user explicitly opts in.

## Plan rationale

Free must be useful enough to become a daily habit and should not gate local features that have no meaningful per-user service cost. Pro is subscription-only because encrypted sync, recovery, storage, support, and hosted Claude usage create ongoing cost.

The initial Pro price is $7 monthly or $56 yearly. Claude use must be a transparent monthly allowance, never an unlimited promise.

## Team roadmap

Team is not sold yet. Shared collections are a Pro capability: a Pro owner can share deliberately selected content with Free or Pro recipients. A future Team plan must add organization-level value—workspace ownership, roles, centralized billing, offboarding, pooled AI usage, and later enterprise controls such as SSO and SCIM.

Team must never mean uploading or searching every employee's private clipboard history.

## Open decisions

- Encryption key recovery and device-loss recovery model
- Claude allowance, overage policy, and supported actions
- Encrypted storage limits and retention policy
- Team workspace roles, key distribution, and administrator recovery controls
- Editorial workflow after the blog begins publishing; the website currently uses localized placeholders and no CMS

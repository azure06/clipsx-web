## Before you begin

Choose your OS and architecture on the [download page](https://clipsx.app/en/download): Windows x64, macOS Apple Silicon or Intel, or Linux/X11 x64. Check actual available release information.

Accounts, extensions, and Ollama are optional. Start with ordinary text search.

## Capture and reuse your first clip {#first-clip}

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install">Use the available package for your platform and its installation instructions. Linux Wayland is outside advertised support.</Step>
  <Step title="Copy">Copy a harmless short note or link from another application.</Step>
  <Step title="Find">Open history with the configured shortcut. Browse or search for a word from the note.</Step>
  <Step title="Reuse">Inspect available representations and choose one to copy or paste. Formats depend on the source application.</Step>
</Steps>

The first workflow succeeds when your note appears and you can reuse it. Continue with [Core workflows](/core-workflows).

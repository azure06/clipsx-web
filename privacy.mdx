## Device-local clipboard data

Clipboard content, managed files, OCR, full-text and semantic indexes, provider
endpoints and models, credentials, permission grants, diagnostics, and derived
data remain on your device. ClipsX does not silently upload them.

## Optional account data

If you create an account, Supabase processes authentication identity, email,
sessions, and hosted records required for supported settings sync. Current sync
does not include clipboard content, notes, tags, files, or secrets.

Google and GitHub process data only when you choose their sign-in options.
Stripe processes billing information only for legacy subscriptions or if a paid
plan is enabled in the future; ClipsX does not store card details. Ollama runs
separately on your device.

## Delete your data

You can close your account from Account settings. Active subscriptions must be
canceled and owned organizations transferred first. Account closure revokes
hosted sessions and removes or disables hosted account data according to
integrity and security requirements.

<Warning>
  Closing an account does not remove data stored by the desktop app. Delete
  local desktop data separately on every device.
</Warning>

<Note>
  The public privacy policy is currently a launch draft and requires final legal
  review and operator identity before production use.
</Note>

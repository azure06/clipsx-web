## Before you begin

ClipsX installers are available for Windows x64, macOS Apple Silicon and Intel,
and Linux/X11 x64. Choose the matching package on the
[download page](https://clipsx.app/en/download).

<Info>
  An account is optional for the core desktop product. Sign in only when you
  want supported settings sync or other account services.
</Info>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install for your device">
    Open the [download page](https://clipsx.app/en/download) and choose your OS
    and architecture. Windows uses a signed `.exe`; macOS uses a signed,
    notarized `.dmg`; Linux uses AppImage or `.deb` on X11. Wayland is outside
    this release's support.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy supported content">
    Copy text or a link normally from an application on your device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open ClipsX">
    Use the configured shortcut to open clipboard history, then search or browse
    for the captured clip.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a representation">
    Preview the representations ClipsX retained, such as rich content and plain
    text, then choose the form you want to paste or copy.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can start without an account, extensions, or local AI. Add those optional
features when you need them. For upgrade limitations and recovery steps, read
the [release notes](https://clipsx.app/en/changelog).

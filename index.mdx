ClipsX is a free desktop clipboard. Find what you copied, inspect its forms, and reuse it. The core app needs no account, extensions, or AI.

## Know the boundaries

Original history is stored on your device. Optional models and extensions process text for enabled tasks; account services have a separate role. Read [privacy](/privacy), [settings sync](/sync), and [platform support](/platform-support).

See [Core workflows](/core-workflows) for everyday use or [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting) for recovery.
